# v0.2 Renderer Compatibility Contract

## Authority and candidate binding

The validator admits only `herdrops-rec-all-v2`, canonical payload SHA-256 `48474610D2A20EE2F7CA2DAC0A3CCF45F919440C9C5D81EF5BA93AD7E524F62D`, reference-host profile SHA-256 `96D01ED15A536F2DF50B59B43CFDEB3683DCE8667AE2E7BF6A96124182FE13A3`, renderer-policy SHA-256 `1D37C9C39449556EB30F9AB5B734F0C5411CF4203321AF0B238993D017229E92`, and package-receipt-schema SHA-256 `8C7EF64ED06C94C6589D73C0AB47EB60B7EEF7BFE337F126D8D9D3F0CC0F4C4B`. `herdrops-rec-all-v1` is superseded and must fail. One manifest binds one exact clean source commit/tree, package identity receipt, ZIP and extracted package manifest, App/Core bytes, reference-host profile, process-wide `SoftwareOnly` policy, exact producer/App report, captures, immutable references, comparison results, matrices, performance/soak samples, and review record. Any missing, malformed, stale, cross-candidate, or changed binding fails closed.

The validator must invoke the package-identity validator against the exact receipt, archive, extracted package root, repository, and profile. It recomputes receipt/leaf identities and does not accept a manifest's PASS assertions as proof. Every evidence file is confined below the evidence root, rejects reparse/path escape, is held or restability-checked against time-of-check/time-of-use changes, and is read as strict bounded content. PNG validation covers the complete file structure and dimensions from the same admitted bytes rather than accepting a header alone.

## Renderer and visual acceptance

Process-wide WPF `SoftwareOnly` must be observed before the first HWND and throughout startup, capture, Dashboard/Widget lifecycle, comparison, and measurement. The renderer observations must bind the exact producer/App report and package components.

Thai and English use separate complete capture sets. References are immutable `docs/design/reference/*.png` bytes. Pixels match exactly outside masks. Masks must be a bounded one-pixel anti-aliasing set declared in an owner/time/hash-bound receipt before capture; no post-hoc mask is allowed. Inside masks, maximum per-channel delta is 8 and changed area is at most 0.1 percent. Every difference needs an explicit disposition. Semantic, layout, glyph, clipping, status, language-separation, or HerdrOps brand failure is a hard FAIL. Narrator and every declared accessibility check are mandatory.

Display coverage is 1920x1080 and 1366x768 at 100, 125, and 150 percent scaling. Mixed-DPI coverage is 100<->150 and 125<->150 in both directions, including primary switch and unplug. Supported Runtime scope is Windows 11 x64 build 26220, the exact governed hardware/profile, local physical console, non-elevated single-user, and both AC and battery. RDP, VM Runtime, ARM64, remote/cloud, and multi-user are excluded; VM is clean-install-only and never earns Runtime credit.

## Performance and soak

Hardware/SoftwareOnly comparison holds candidate content, workload, host, and session constant and varies renderer policy only. Each AB and BA order has one warm-up plus five measured repetitions. Raw samples and their canonical identities are mandatory and all aggregates are recomputed. Limits are CPU <=1 percent, event-to-WPF p95 <=250 ms, SoftwareOnly CPU regression <=10 percent and <=0.50 percentage point, latency regression <=10 percent, UI-stall p95 <=50 ms, and UI-stall maximum <=100 ms. A missing or invalid sample fails.

Soak is 60 minutes on AC plus 60 minutes on battery in five-minute bins. The combined working-set maximum is <=255 MiB (`267386880` bytes) only under the exact v0.2 profile and the resource slope is <=1 MiB (`1048576` bytes) per ten minutes. All bins, raw samples, power observations, hashes, and recomputed statistics are required; a missing bin/sample fails. Critical invariants cannot be waived. Any later performance waiver must cite exact Plan authority, bind one successor candidate, and never acts retroactively.

## Review and evidence boundary

Agents may build or operate but cannot grant ProductOwner or final Human authority. A candidate needs role-distinct Human review with every visual difference dispositioned and no open High/Critical security finding. CI self-tests are Static, Contract, and Synthetic verifier evidence only. A validator PASS without candidate observations is at most `PackagedCompatibilityCandidate`; it is not actual Herdr Runtime, clean-install, Human GO, Release, Beta/Stable GO, tag, signing, or publication authority. Issue closure and any merge require exact-head CI, required review, and issue-local evidence. Rebuilt or changed bytes require fresh validation and review.
