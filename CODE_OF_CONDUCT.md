# HerdrOps Community Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the HerdrOps community respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, education, appearance, ability, nationality, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and observable behavior.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Protect private information and never pressure another person to publish identifying or sensitive data.
- Use the project's evidence labels honestly; do not represent synthetic or contract results as actual runtime or release proof.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project-managed communication spaces, and when a person publicly represents the HerdrOps project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For immediate safety or platform abuse, use GitHub's **Report abuse** controls. Do not publish personal details or a conduct report in a public issue. If project-maintainer review is needed and no private contact channel is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact without describing the incident or naming participants.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants whose behavior is inconsistent with this code. Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as the available channel permits, and retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
